1. On the **Overview**, find *Case team*.
2. On the person's card, press their role, under their name (for example **Support ▾**).
3. Choose the new role from the list. Their current role is marked.

A delegate's role (*Representative*) can't be changed here: it shows as plain text.

The new role shows straight away and saves. If it couldn't save, the old role comes back and the card says *Not saved. Try again.*

> **Tip:** The role says what they do on *this* case, so the same person can have a different role on another case. The list's headings, *Case team* and *Delegates*, come from who they are and can't be changed here. To make someone responsible for the case, [make them lead](#change-the-lead-staff) with the crown.
