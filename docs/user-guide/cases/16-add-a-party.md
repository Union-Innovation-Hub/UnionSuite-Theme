*Parties* are everyone the case is about: members and employers.

1. Open the **Parties** tab and press **Add party**.
2. Start typing a name. The list shows members at the case's workplace.
3. Pick the person and press **Add**.
4. The box stays open so you can add the next person. Press **Add party** again to close it.

People already on the case show in the list but cannot be picked.

**Not in the list?** Press **More options** to search everyone, including employers.
