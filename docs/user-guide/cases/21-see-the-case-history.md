The Overview's *Recent activity* shows the last 30 days. For everything:

1. Open the **Activity** tab, or press **View all activity** on the Overview.
2. *All activity* lists notes, emails, meetings and files, newest first.
3. To narrow it:
   - Choose a type: **Notes**, **Emails**, **Meetings** or **Attachments**.
   - Type in the search box.
   - Choose a timeframe, such as 90 days or All time.
4. Select a card to see more. An email shows who it was from and to; press **View email** to read it.

> **Tip:** With Notes or Meetings chosen, press **Details view** for a detailed list instead of the timeline. Press it again to go back.
