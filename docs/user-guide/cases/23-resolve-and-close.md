Recording the outcome and closing the case are one step.

1. Choose **Resolve case**. It is in the status menu, the banner's **Actions** menu and the *Resolution* panel on the **Details** tab.
2. If tasks are still open, the form says how many. They stay open after the case closes.
3. If the form asks for a **Status**, choose the one the case closes with. Sites with only one closing status don't show it.
4. Choose the **Outcome type**. **Closed date** starts as today; press **Yesterday** or pick another date if it closed earlier.
5. If anything was won, fill in **Amount awarded** (dollars), **Time awarded** (weeks, such as personal leave), or both. Leave them empty if nothing was.
6. Say what happened under **Outcome**.
7. Press **Resolve case**, or `Ctrl+Enter`.

The page reloads. The case is closed, and the Overview now starts with the resolution.

> **Tip:** Finish or tidy up open tasks first, so nothing is left hanging.
