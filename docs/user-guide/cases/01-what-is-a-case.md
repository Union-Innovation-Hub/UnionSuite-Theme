A case keeps everything about one issue in one place:

- **The member** it is about, and anyone else involved (the *parties*).
- **The staff** working on it, with one person as the *lead staff*.
- **Notes, tasks, meetings, emails and files** added as you work.
- **The outcome**, once the case is resolved.

Most cases are one staff member helping one member. Some have several members, or an employer, as well.
