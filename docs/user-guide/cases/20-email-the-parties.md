1. Open the **Parties** tab.
2. To email only some people, press **Select** and tick them, or search to narrow the list. To email everyone, skip this step.
3. Check the line above the list. It says who the email goes to.
4. Press **Email parties** (the send icon).
5. Write and send the email on the page that opens.

To email everyone, you can also choose **Email parties** from the banner's **Actions** menu.

The subject starts with the case number, and the email is saved to the case. You find it on the **Activity** tab under *Emails*.
