1. Press **Add note**. You find it on the Overview's *Recent activity*, and on the **Activity** tab.
2. Write the note.
3. Choose a **Category** if you need one.
4. To follow it up later, tick **Follow up** and choose the **Follow-up date**. The note then shows as a task too.
5. Press **Save note**, or press `Ctrl+Enter`.

You see a tick and *Saved*, and the note appears at the top.

**Good to know**

- Press **Add note** again to put the note away. What you wrote is kept for next time.
- **Cancel** or `Esc` throws the note away.
- **More options** opens the full note form, with your text already in it. Use it to assign the note to people.
