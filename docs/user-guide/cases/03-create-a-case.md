A case always belongs to a member, so start from their profile.

1. Open the member's profile.
2. Press **Create case**.
3. Choose the **Type** and **Subtype**.
4. If the member has more than one job, choose the **Workplace** the case is about.
5. Choose the **Lead staff**, or press **Assign me to this case** to take it yourself.
6. Check the **Priority**. It starts as Normal.
7. Choose who can see the case under **Access**: Open, Team or Individual. See [Who can see a case](#who-can-see-a-case).
8. Press **Create case**. The new case opens.

> **Tip:** If you choose Individual access, someone must be on the case: choose a lead staff member or assign yourself.

**If something goes wrong**

- *Nothing was saved:* the form stays open with a message. Fix what it says and try again.
- *Saved, but a step was missed:* the message has a link to the new case. Open it and check the details.

Some fields may be hidden or required, depending on how your organisation has set up the form.
