**Top line:** the case number, the title, the **status** and the **Actions** menu.

**Under the title:** the member's name (select it to open their profile), their member number, and a badge if they are:

- **Unfinancial**,
- **No longer a member**, or
- a **New member** (joined in the last three months).

**Second line:** the description, then:

| Fact | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Opened** | The date, and how many days the case has been open. Once closed, how long it took. |
| **Type** and **Subtype** | What kind of case it is. |
| **Priority** | How urgent it is. |
| **Lead staff** | Who is responsible. Red *No lead staff assigned* means nobody is. |
| **Last member contact** | The last note, email, call or meeting with the member. Amber after 14 days; red after 28. |

> **Tip:** As you scroll, the banner shrinks but keeps the member's name in view.
