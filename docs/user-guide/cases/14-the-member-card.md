The member card is at the top of the Overview's side column. It shows:

- The member's name and member number. Press **View profile** to open their record.
- A **warning** if they are unfinancial, no longer a member, or joined in the last three months. Check it before acting for them.
- Their contact, membership and work details.
- The names of anyone else on the case, with **See all parties**.

> **Tip:** The same warning shows as a badge beside the member's name in the banner.
