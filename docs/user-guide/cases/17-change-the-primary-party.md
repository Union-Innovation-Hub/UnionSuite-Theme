When a case has several members, one is the *primary party*. They show in the banner and on the member card.

1. Open the **Parties** tab.
2. Choose the member in the list.
3. Press the crown beside their name: **Make primary party**.

The amber crown moves to them, they move to the top of the list, and the banner and member card change to them.

> **Tip:** Only members can be the primary party, not employers.
