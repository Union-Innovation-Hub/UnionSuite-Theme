1. Press **Add meeting**. You find it on the Overview's *Meetings*, and on the **Activity** tab.
2. Type the **Meeting title**.
3. Choose the **Meeting date**.
4. Add the **Start time** and **Location** if you know them.
5. Press **Save meeting**.

The meeting joins the list in date order.

**Good to know**

- If the date has already passed, you are asked to check. Press **Save anyway** to keep it.
- **More options** opens the full form, for attendees and an agenda.
