UNIONSUITE / DESIGN WORKSHOP 02

Help, one click away.

A side pane from the right of the taskbar, with a help assistant and a support ticket form. It runs on the theme’s own header, taskbar, bookmarks and Recents.

Work in progress
When the pane opens
01

Help & support pane

Draft

Select the side pane toggle at the far right of the taskbar. Ask a question, or switch to Support ticket. When an answer doesn't solve it, Raise a ticket carries the chat across so you don't explain it twice.

Try it
1

One place to get unstuck

The pane opens beside the work, not over it in a popup, so you can follow the steps while you do them. It stays open until you close it.

2

Ask first, then escalate

The assistant answers from the usage guide and shows where the answer came from. When it can't help, the ticket starts with the chat attached.

3

Context comes with you

The ticket includes the page and browser automatically, each one can be unticked, and you can add a screenshot. Replies go to your iMIS email.
