Resolve case form

One form: resolve, or edit the resolution

  1. Resolving and closing are one step (C16). Saving records the outcome and closes the case; the page reloads with the resolution first on the Overview.
  2. Open tasks are said in the form, above the fields, instead of a browser confirm before it opens. They stay open after the case closes, as now. One less dialog, and the warning is still there when you save.
  3. Outcome type and Closed date are required. The date starts as today, with Today and Yesterday to hand, and can't be in the future or before the case was opened.
  4. Amount awarded (dollars) and Time awarded (weeks, such as personal leave) are separate and both optional, so a case can win both. They start empty: blank means nothing awarded and saves as empty, never 0. Cents and part weeks (1.5) are fine. They are the live ResolutionAmount and ResolutionWeeks.
  5. Outcome says what happened in words, for the next person to read.
  6. Edit resolution is the same form, filled in, with who resolved it and when. Save changes keeps the case closed.
  7. Ctrl+Enter saves from anywhere in the form; Esc or Cancel closes it without saving.

Last action

Resolve case or Cancel to see what the form would send and how it would close.
